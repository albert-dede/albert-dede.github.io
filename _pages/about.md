## A Bit About Me

I am currently a PhD researcher specialising in Artificial Intelligence at [Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST)](https://www.knust.edu.gh/) in Ghana. I am passionate about machine learning and computer vision. I want to advance scientific research by using new methods in representation learning and deep learning.

At the [Responsible Artificial Intelligence Lab](https://rail.knust.edu.gh/), I work on advanced AI projects while I pursue my doctoral studies, which I expect to complete in November 2025. My research mainly focuses on developing novel machine learning methods for image processing. I aim to improve medical imaging and diagnostics using computer vision technologies.

## Academics

My academic journey has been very much a blend of technical and interdisciplinary learning:
* *PhD in Computer Engineering* (KNUST, 2022-2025 expected)

* *Master of Philosophy in Computer Engineering* (KNUST, 2020-2022)

## Professional Experience

### Research Leadership
I design and implement various AI projects, manage complex datasets, and contribute to research publications. My work often involves analysing gigapixel images and developing weakly supervised methods for computer vision applications.

### Teaching Experience
I have had the opportunity to contribute to KNUST’s Department of Computer Engineering in several teaching roles, including:
- Serving as a Graduate Teaching Assistant for Communication Systems and Information Theory.
- Working as a Teaching Assistant for Microprocessors and Digital Signal Processing courses.
- Supervising labs and organising tutorials for some undergraduate courses.

## Research Contributions

I have authored and co-authored several [peer-reviewed publications](http://albert-dede.github.io/publications/) in respected journals.

Additionally, I serve as a reviewer for leading journals, including Expert Systems with Applications and Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence.

## Professional Engagement

I actively engage with the global AI community by attending top conferences like [PyCon Africa](https://africa.pycon.org/2024/) and [Deep Learning Indaba](https://deeplearningindaba.com/2023/), and participating in specialised workshops such as the [African Computer Vision Summer School](https://www.acvss.ai/) and [GIS Methods for Quantitative Spatial Data Analysis](https://wheelerblog.london.edu/geographic-information-system-workshop/). ## Personal Mission

I am passionate about using artificial intelligence to solve real problems, especially in medical imaging and diagnostics. My goal is to connect research with practical applications that can truly benefit society.
